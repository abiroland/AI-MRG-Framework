# Project Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** merge pull requests, cut releases and set the roadmap.
  - Roland Abi ([@abiroland](https://github.com/abiroland)), founder and lead maintainer
- **Pack reviewers** are practitioners (model validators, compliance and fair-lending professionals) who review changes to regulatory content in `packs/`. Before v1.0 we aim to have at least three.
- **Contributors**: anyone who opens an issue or pull request.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes: lazy consensus on the pull request.
- Significant design changes: an architecture decision record in `docs/adr/`, open for comment for at least 7 days.
- Pack content changes need approval from a maintainer **and**, once pack reviewers exist, from one pack reviewer.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contributions over several months, followed by nomination by an existing maintainer.
