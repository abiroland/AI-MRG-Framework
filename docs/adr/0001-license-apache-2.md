# ADR-0001: License under Apache 2.0

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-09

## Context
The goal is adoption by banks, credit unions, fintechs and researchers with no licensing cost. Their legal teams scrutinize open-source licenses. Source-available licenses (for example BSL) and strong copyleft (AGPL) often slow or block internal approval.

## Decision
Use the **Apache License 2.0** for code and packs.

## Consequences
- OSI-approved and permissive, so it is widely pre-approved by corporate legal teams.
- It includes an explicit patent grant, which MIT does not, and that is valued by regulated institutions.
- Others may build commercial products on it. That is acceptable, and it supports the goal of wide adoption.
- Contributions are accepted under the same license (inbound = outbound), with a DCO sign-off and no CLA.
