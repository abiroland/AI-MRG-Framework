# Use statement template

Some organizations that pilot or adopt AI-MRG are willing to provide a short written
statement of how they used it. This template keeps that statement **factual**. Write it
in your own words; the bracketed prompts are guidance only. State only what is true,
leave out anything you are unsure of, and have it reviewed under your organization's
usual approval process.

---

[Organization letterhead, if your organization permits]

[Date]

**Re: Use of the AI-MRG Framework**

1. **Who we are.** [Organization, sector, approximate size. Your name, role, and how you
   were involved.]

2. **What we used.** [Version(s) of AI-MRG, which components (for example inventory and
   tiering, automated checks, fair-lending or adverse-action checks, lifecycle workflow,
   reports, dashboard, examination packages), and over what period.]

3. **How we used it.** [The models or AI systems involved, described generically if
   needed; pilot or production use; on what infrastructure.]

4. **What we observed.** [Concrete, measurable observations where possible, for example
   time saved on a report, issues detected, controls evidenced. Include limitations or
   problems you encountered.]

5. **Our current status.** [Continuing to use it, expanding, evaluating, or stopped, and
   why.]

[Name]
[Title, Organization]
[Contact information, if you wish]

---

*Notes for writers:* There is no expectation of praise. A balanced statement, including
limitations, is more useful and more credible. Do not include confidential information,
customer data, or examination findings.
